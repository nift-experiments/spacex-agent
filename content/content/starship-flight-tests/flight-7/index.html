January 16, 2025
On its seventh flight test, Starship successfully lifted off from Starbase in Texas at 4:37 p.m. CT on Thursday, January 16. The Super Heavy booster maneuvered itself back to the launch and catch tower, resulting in the second successful catch of a booster. Telemetry was lost with Starship prior to end of its ascent burn, with initial data indicating a fire in the aft section of the ship.
