August 05, 2014

AsiaSat 8 Mission

REWATCH

On Tuesday, August 5 at 4:00 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the AsiaSat 8 mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the first and final flight for the Falcon 9 booster supporting this mission.
