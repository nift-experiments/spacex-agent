December 21, 2015

ORBCOMM-2 Mission

Rewatch

On Monday, December 21, at 8:29 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the ORBCOMM-2 mission to low Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

Following stage separation, the Falcon 9 first stage successfully returned to Earth and landed at Landing Zone 1 at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida, completing the world’s first ever recovery of an orbital class rocket.
