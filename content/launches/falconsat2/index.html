March 24, 2006

FalconSAT-2 Mission

On Friday, March 24 at 7:00 p.m. MHT, Falcon 1 attempted to launch the FalconSAT-2 mission to low-Earth orbit from the SpaceX launch site on Omelek Island in the Marshall Islands.

This was the first and final flight of the Falcon 1 booster supporting this mission.
