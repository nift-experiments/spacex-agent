February 27, 2023

STARLINK MISSION

REWATCH

On Monday, February 27 at 6:13 p.m. ET, SpaceX launched 21 second-generation Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the third launch and landing of this Falcon 9 first stage booster, which previously launched CRS-26 and OneWeb Launch 16.
