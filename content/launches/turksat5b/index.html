December 18, 2021

Turksat 5B Mission

Rewatch

On Saturday, December 18 at 10:58 p.m. EST, Falcon 9 launched the Turksat 5B mission to a Geostationary Transfer Orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the third launch and landing of this booster, which previously supported launch of CRS-22 and Crew-3.
