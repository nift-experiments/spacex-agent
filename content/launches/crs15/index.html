June 29, 2018

CRS-15 Mission

REWATCH

On Friday, June 29 at 5:42 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 15th Commercial Resupply Services (CRS-15) mission to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately three days later on Monday, July 2, the spacecraft was captured at 6:54 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 9:50 a.m. ET.

After 31 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 12:38 p.m. ET on August 3, 2018, and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 3:17 p.m. PT.

This was the second flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched TESS.
