March 21, 2007

DemoSat Mission

On Wednesday, March 21 at 1:10 p.m. MHT, Falcon 1 attempted to launch the DemoSat mission to low-Earth orbit from the SpaceX launch site on Omelek Island in the Marshall Islands.

This was the first and final flight of the Falcon 1 booster supporting this mission.
