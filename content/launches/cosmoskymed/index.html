January 31, 2022

COSMO-SkyMed Second Generation FM2 Mission

Rewatch

On Thursday, January 27 at 6:11 p.m. EST, Falcon 9 launched the COSMO-SkyMed Second Generation FM2 mission to low Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the third launch and landing of this booster, which previously supported the launch of Arabsat-6A and STP-2.
