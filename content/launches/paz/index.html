February 22, 2018

Paz Mission

REWATCH

On Thursday, February 22 at 6:17 a.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched the PAZ mission to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 4E (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Air Force Base in California.

This was the second and final flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched FORMOSAT-5.
