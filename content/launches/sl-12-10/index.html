April 28, 2025

Starlink Mission

Watch

On Monday, April 28 at 10:34 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 23 Starlink satellites, including 13 with Direct to Cell capabilities, to low-Earth orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission.
