June 25, 2017

Iridium-2 Mission

Rewatch

On Sunday, June 25, at 1:25 p.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched the Iridium-2 mission to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 4E (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Air Force Base in California.

This was the first flight for the Falcon 9 first stage booster. It successfully landed on the Just Read the Instructions droneship.
