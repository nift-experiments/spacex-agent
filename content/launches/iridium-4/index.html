December 23, 2017

Iridium-4 Mission

Rewatch

On Saturday, December 23, at 8:27 p.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched the Iridium-4 mission to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 4E (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Air Force Base in California.

This was the second flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched Iridium-2. There was no attempt to recover the first stage booster supporting this mission.
