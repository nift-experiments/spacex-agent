September 29, 2013

Cassiope Mission

On Sunday, September 29 at 9:00 a.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched the Cassiope mission to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 4 East (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Air Force Base in California, completing SpaceX's first launch from SLC-4E.

This was the first SpaceX launch from California.
