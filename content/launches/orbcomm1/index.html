July 14, 2014

ORBCOMM-1 Mission

On Monday, July 14 at 11:15 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Orbcomm-1 mission to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the first and final flight for the Falcon 9 booster supporting this mission.
