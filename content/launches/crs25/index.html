July 14, 2022

CRS-25 MISSION

REWATCH

On Thursday, July 14 at 8:44 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon's 25th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-25) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center, Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 11:21 a.m. ET on Saturday, July 16.

After 33 days, SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft undocked from the orbiting laboratory and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Saturday, August 20 at 2:53 p.m. ET.

This was the third flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew CRS-21 and CRS-23 to and from the space station. This was the fifth flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched CRS-22, Crew-3, Turksat 5B, and Crew-4.
