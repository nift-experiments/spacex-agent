GPS III-9 Mission

WATCH

On Tuesday, January 27 at 11:53 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the GPS III-9 mission to medium-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the fifth flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched KF-01, IMAP, NROL-77, and one Starlink mission. Following stage separation, the first stage landed on the A Shortfall of Gravitas droneship, which was stationed in the Atlantic Ocean.
