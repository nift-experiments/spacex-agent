August 29, 2021

CRS-23 MISSION

REWATCH

On Sunday, August 29 at 3:14 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon's 23rd Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-23) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center, Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 10:30 a.m. ET on Monday, August 30.

After 30 days, SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft undocked from the orbiting laboratory and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Thursday, September 30 at 10:57 p.m. ET.

This was the second flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew CRS-21 to and from the space station. This was the fourth flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched Crew-1, Crew-2, and SXM-8.
