June 25, 2025

AX-4 MISSION

REWATCH

On Wednesday, June 25 at 2:31 a.m. ET, SpaceX’s Falcon 9 launched Axiom Space’s Axiom Mission 4 (Ax-4) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the space station at 6:31 a.m. ET on Thursday, June 26.

After 20 days in space, Dragon and the Ax-4 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Tuesday, July 15 at 5:31 a.m. PT.

This was the first flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting the mission. This was the second flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched a Starlink mission.
