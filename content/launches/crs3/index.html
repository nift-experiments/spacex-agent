April 18, 2014

CRS-3 Mission

On Friday, April 18 at 3:25 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s third Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-3) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Sunday, April 20 the spacecraft was captured at 7:14 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 10:06 a.m. ET.

After 27 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 7:55 a.m. ET on Sunday, May 18 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 12:05 p.m. PT.
