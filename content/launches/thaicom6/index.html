January 06, 2014

Thaicom-6 Mission

On Monday, January 6 at 5:06 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Thaicom-6 mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the first and final flight for the Falcon 9 booster supporting this mission.
