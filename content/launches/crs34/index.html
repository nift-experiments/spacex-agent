CRS-34 Mission

WATCH

After 30 days docked to the International Space Station, SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft completed its 34th Commercial Resupply Services mission to and from the International Space Station for NASA, successfully returning to Earth and splashing down off the coast of California on Wednesday, June 17 at 5:11 a.m. PT.

CRS-34 was the sixth flight of the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew CRS-22, CRS-24, CRS-27, CRS-30, and CRS-32 to and from the space station.

Falcon 9 launched Dragon to the orbiting laboratory on Friday, May 15, 2026 at 6:05 p.m. ET from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.
