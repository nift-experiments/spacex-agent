June 25, 2025

Starlink Mission

REWATCH

On Wednesday, June 25 at 3:54 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 27 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the 20th flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched Ax-2, Euclid, Ax-3, CRS-30, SES ASTRA 1P, NG-21, and now 14 Starlink missions.
