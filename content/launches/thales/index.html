April 27, 2015

Thales Mission

On Monday, April 27 at 7:23 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Thales mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the first and final flight for the Falcon 9 booster supporting this mission.
