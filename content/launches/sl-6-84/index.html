May 04, 2025

Starlink Mission

REWATCH

On Sunday, May 4 at 4:54 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 29 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

This was the 20th flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched Crew-6, SES O3b mPOWER-B, USSF-124, Bluebird 1-5, and now 16 Starlink missions.
