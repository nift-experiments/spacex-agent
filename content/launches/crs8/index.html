CRS-8 Mission

Rewatch

On Friday, April 8, at 8:43 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s eighth Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-8) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Sunday, April 10, the spacecraft was captured at 7:23 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 9:57 a.m. ET.

After 30 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 9:19 a.m. ET on Wednesday, May 11 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California later that day at 11:31 a.m. PT.
