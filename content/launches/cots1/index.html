December 08, 2010

COTS 1 Mission

On Wednesday, December 8 at 10:43 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s first demonstration mission under NASA’s Commercial Orbital Transportation Services program (COTS 1) to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft separated from Falcon 9 just over nine minutes into flight and achieved a nominal orbit. The spacecraft successfully returned to Earth and splashed down off the coast of Baja, California less than four hours later at 11:02 a.m. PT.
