On Wednesday, March 29 at 4:01 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 56 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, Florida

This was the fourth launch and landing for this Falcon 9 first stage booster, which previously launched Crew-5, GPS III Space Vehicle 06, and Inmarsat I-6 F2.
