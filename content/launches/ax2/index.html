May 21, 2023

Ax-2 Mission

REWATCH

On Sunday, May 21 at 5:37 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched launched Dragon and Axiom Space's Axiom Mission 2 (Ax-2) to the orbiting laboratory from Launch Complex 39A at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the International Space Station on Monday, May 22 at 9:12 a.m. ET.

After 10 days in space, Dragon and the Ax-3 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida at 11:04 p.m. ET on Tuesday, May 30.

This was the second flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew NASA’s Crew-4 mission to and from the space station. This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission. Following stage separation, Falcon 9’s first stage landed on Landing Zone 1 (LZ-1) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

Ax-2 was Axiom Space’s second private astronaut mission to the International Space Station, and the second of four missions SpaceX has launched to the space station on behalf of Axiom Space via SpaceX’s Falcon 9 and Dragon vehicles.
