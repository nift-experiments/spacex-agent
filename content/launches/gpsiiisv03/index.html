June 30, 2020

GPS III Space Vehicle 03 MISSION

REWATCH

On Tuesday, June 30th at 4:10 p.m. ET, 20:10 UTC, SpaceX's Falcon 9 successfully lifted off, carrying the Space Force's GPS III Space Vehicle 03 mission from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

Following stage separation, SpaceX landed Falcon 9’s first stage on the “Just Read the Instructions” droneship, stationed in the Atlantic Ocean. The spacecraft deployed approximately 1 hour and 29 minutes after liftoff.
