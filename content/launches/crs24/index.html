December 21, 2021

CRS-24 MISSION

REWATCH

On Tuesday, December 21 at 5:06 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon's 24th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-24) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center, Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 3:41 a.m. ET on Wednesday, December 22.

After 32 days, SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft undocked from the orbiting laboratory and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Monday, January 24 at 4:05 p.m. ET.

This was the second flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew CRS-22 to and from the space station. This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission.
