March 06, 2020

CRS-20 MISSION

REWATCH

On Friday, March 6 at 11:50 p.m. EST, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s twentieth Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-20) from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station, Florida. Approximately two days later on Monday, March 9, the spacecraft was captured at 6:25 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 7:18 a.m. ET.

After 28 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 6:30 a.m. ET on Tuesday, April 7 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 11:50 a.m. PT.

The Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission previously CRS-10 and CRS-16. This was the second flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched CRS-19. This mission also marked the 50th landing of an orbital class rocket booster and final flight of the first iteration of the Dragon spacecraft.
