May 09, 2024

Starlink Mission

REWATCH

On Thursday, May 9 at 9:30 p.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched 20 Starlink satellites, including 13 with Direct to Cell capabilities, to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 4 East (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California.

This was the fourth flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched USSF-62, and now three Starlink missions.
