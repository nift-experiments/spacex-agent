September 07, 2014

AsiaSat 6 Mission

On Sunday, September 7 at 1:00 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the AsiaSat 6 mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the first and final flight for the Falcon 9 booster supporting this mission.
