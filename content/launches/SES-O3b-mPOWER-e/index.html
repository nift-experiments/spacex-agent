December 17, 2024

SES O3b mPOWER Mission

Rewatch

On Tuesday, December 17 at 5:26 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the SES O3b mPOWER mission to medium Earth orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission.
