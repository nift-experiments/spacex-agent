August 26, 2023

Crew-7 Mission

REWATCH

On Saturday, August 26 at 3:27 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s seventh operational human spaceflight mission Crew-7 to the orbiting laboratory from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at NASA's Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon autonomously docked with the International Space Station approximately 30 hours later at 9:16 a.m. ET on Sunday, August 27.

After 199 days in space, Dragon and the Crew-7 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida at 5:47 a.m. ET on Tuesday, March 12.

This was the third flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew NASA’s Crew-3 and Crew-5 missions to and from the space station. This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission. Following stage separation, Falcon 9’s first stage landed on Landing Zone 1 (LZ-1) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.

During their time on the orbiting laboratory, the crew conducted science and technology demonstrations to prepare for human exploration beyond low-Earth orbit and to benefit humanity on Earth.
