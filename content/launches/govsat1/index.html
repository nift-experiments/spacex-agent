January 31, 2018

GovSat-1 Mission

REWATCH

On Wednesday, January 31 at 4:25 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the GovSat-1 mission to geostationary transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the second flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched NROL-76.
