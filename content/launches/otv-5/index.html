Orbital Test Vehicle 5 (OTV-5) Mission

Rewatch

On Thursday, September 7, at 10:00 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Orbital Test Vehicle 5 (OTV-5) mission to orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

This was the first flight for the Falcon 9 first stage booster supporting this mission. It successfully landed on Landing Zone-1 at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.
