October 11, 2017

EchoStar 105 / SES-11 Mission

REWATCH

On Wednesday, October 11, at 6:53 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the EchoStar 105 / SES-11 mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

This was the second flight for the Falcon 9 first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched CRS-10. It successfully landed on the Of Course I Still Love You droneship.
