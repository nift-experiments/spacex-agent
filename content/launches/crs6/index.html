April 14, 2015

CRS-6 Mission

On Tuesday, April 14 at 8:10 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s sixth Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-6) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Friday, April 17 the spacecraft was captured at 5:55 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 9:29 a.m. ET.

After 33 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 5:29 a.m. ET on Thursday, May 21 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 9:42 p.m. PT.
