June 23, 2023

Starlink Mission

REWATCH

On Friday, June 23 at 11:35 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 56 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the eighth launch and landing for this Falcon 9 first stage booster, which previously launched CRS-24, Eutelsat HOTBIRD 13F, OneWeb 1, SES-18 and SES-19, and now four Starlink missions.
