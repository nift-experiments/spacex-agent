May 22, 2018

Iridium-6/GRACE-FO Mission

On Tuesday, May 22 at 12:47 p.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched Iridium-6/GRACE-FO to low-Earth Orbit from Space Launch Complex 4E (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Air Force Base in California.

This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission.
