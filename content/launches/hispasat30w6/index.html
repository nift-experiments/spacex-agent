March 06, 2018

HISPASAT 30W-6 Mission

On Tuesday, March 6 at 12:33 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Hispasat 30W-6 mission to geostationary transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the first flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission.
