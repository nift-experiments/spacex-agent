February 26, 2025

Starlink Mission

REWATCH

On Wednesday, February 26 at 10:34 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 21 Starlink satellites, including 13 with Direct to Cell capabilities, to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the first flight of the first stage booster supporting this mission.
