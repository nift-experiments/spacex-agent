July 14, 2009

RazakSAT Mission

On Tuesday, July 14 at 3:35 p.m. MHT, Falcon 1 launched the RazakSAT mission to low-Earth orbit from the SpaceX launch site on Omelek Island in the Marshall Islands.

This was the final flight in the Falcon 1 program.
