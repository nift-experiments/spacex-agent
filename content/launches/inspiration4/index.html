September 15, 2021

Inspiration4 Mission

REWATCH

On Wednesday, September 15 at 10:02 p.m. ET, SpaceX’s Falcon 9 launched Inspiration4 to low-Earth orbit from Launch Complex 39A at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

After three days orbiting Earth, Dragon and the Inspiration4 crew – the world’s first civilian mission to orbit – safely splashed down off the coast of Florida on Saturday, September 18 at 7:06 p.m. ET.

This was the second flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew Crew-1 to and from the space station. This was the third flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously GPS III Space Vehicle 04 and GPS III Space Vehicle 05.
