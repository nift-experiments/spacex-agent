March 09, 2023

ONEWEB LAUNCH 17 MISSION

REWATCH

On Thursday, March 9 at 2:13 p.m. ET, SpaceX launched the OneWeb Launch 17 mission to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the thirteenth launch and landing for this Falcon 9 first stage booster, which previously launched GPS III Space Vehicle 04, GPS III Space Vehicle 05, Inspiration4, Ax-1, Nilesat 301, and seven Starlink missions.
