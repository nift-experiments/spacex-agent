August 03, 2008

Trailblazer Mission

On Sunday, August 3 at 3:34 p.m. MHT, Falcon 1 attempted to launch the Trailblazer mission to low-Earth orbit from the SpaceX launch site on Omelek Island in the Marshall Islands.

This was the first and final flight of the Falcon 1 booster supporting this mission.
