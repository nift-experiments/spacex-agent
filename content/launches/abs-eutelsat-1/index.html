March 01, 2015

ABS/Eutelsat-1 Mission

On Sunday, March 1 at 10:50 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the ABS/Eutesat-1 mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida.

This was the first and final flight for the Falcon 9 booster supporting this mission.
