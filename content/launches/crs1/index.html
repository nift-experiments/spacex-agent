October 07, 2012

CRS-1 Mission

On Sunday, October 7 at 8:34 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s first Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-1) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately three days later on Wednesday, October 10 the spacecraft was captured at 6:56 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 9:03 a.m. ET.

After 17 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 7:19 a.m. ET on Monday, October 28 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 12:22 p.m. PT.
