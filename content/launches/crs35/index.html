October 13, 2026

CRS-35 Mission

WATCH

SpaceX is targeting Tuesday, October 13 for Falcon 9’s launch of Dragon’s 35th Commercial Resupply Services (CRS-35) mission to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida. Liftoff is targeted for 6:33 a.m. ET, with a backup launch opportunity available on Wednesday, October 14 at 6:08 a.m. ET if needed.

A live webcast of this mission will begin about 20 minutes prior to liftoff, which you can watch here and on X @SpaceX.

CRS-35 is the fourth flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew CRS-26, CRS-29, and CRS-33 to and from the space station. After an approximate 52-hour flight, Dragon will autonomously dock with the orbiting laboratory on Thursday, October 15 at approximately 11:00 a.m. ET.

This will be the eighth flight of the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched KF-01, IMAP, NROL-77, GPS III-9, CRS-34, NROL-95, and one Starlink mission. Following stage separation, the first stage will land on Landing Zone 40 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.
