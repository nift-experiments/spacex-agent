February 15, 2024

Intuitive Machines IM-1 Mission

Rewatch

On Thursday, February 15 at 1:05 a.m. ET, SpaceX launched the Intuitive Machines IM-1 mission to lunar transfer orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

This was the 18th flight of the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched GPS III-3, Turksat 5A, Transporter-2, Intelsat G-33/G-34, Transporter-6, and 12 Starlink missions.
