August 24, 2025

CRS-33 Mission

WATCH

After 185 days docked to the International Space Station, SpaceX’s Dragon spacecraft completed its 33rd Commercial Resupply Services (CRS-33) mission to and from the International Space Station for NASA, successfully returning to Earth and splashing down off the coast of California on Thursday, February 26 at 11:45 p.m. PT.

CRS-33 was the third flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew CRS-26 and CRS-29 to and from the space station.

Falcon 9 launched Dragon to the orbiting laboratory on Sunday, August 24, 2025, at 2:45 a.m. ET from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.
