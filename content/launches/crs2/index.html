March 01, 2013

CRS-2 Mission

On Friday, March 1 at 10:10 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s second Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-2) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Sunday, March 3 the spacecraft was captured at 5:31 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 8:56 a.m. ET.

After 22 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 4:10 a.m. ET on Tuesday, March 26 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 9:34 a.m. PT.
