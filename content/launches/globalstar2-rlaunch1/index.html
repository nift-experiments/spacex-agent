Globalstar 2-R Launch 1 Mission

WATCH

On Saturday, August 15 at 9:12 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Globalstar 2-R Launch 1 mission to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the 14th flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously supported SES O3b mPOWER-E, Crew-10, Bandwagon-3, SES O3b mPOWER-D, CRS-33, and eight Starlink missions. Following stage separation, the first stage landed on the Landing Zone 40 (LZ-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station.
