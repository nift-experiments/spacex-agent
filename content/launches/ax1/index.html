April 08, 2022

AX-1 MISSION

REWATCH

On Friday, April 8 at 11:17 a.m. ET, SpaceX’s Falcon 9 launched Axiom Space’s Axiom Mission 1 (Ax-1) to the International Space Station from Launch Complex 39A at NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Dragon docked with the space station at 8:29 a.m. ET on Saturday, April 9.

After 17 days in space, Dragon and the Ax-1 astronauts returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Florida on Monday, April 25 at 1:06 p.m. ET.

This was the third flight for the Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission, which previously flew Demo-2 and Crew-2 to and from the space station. This was the fifth flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched GPS III Space Vehicle 04, GPS III Space Vehicle 05, Inspiration4, and a Starlink mission.
