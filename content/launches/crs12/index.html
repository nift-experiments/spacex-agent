CRS-12 Mission

Rewatch

On Monday, August 14, at 12:31 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 12th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-12) to the International Space Station from NASA’s Kennedy Space Center in Florida. Approximately two days later on Wednesday, August 16, the spacecraft was captured at 6:52 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 9:07 a.m. ET.

After 31 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 4:40 a.m. ET on Sunday, September 17 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California later that day at 7:14 a.m. PT.
