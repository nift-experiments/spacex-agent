April 01, 2022

Transporter-4 Mission

Rewatch

On Friday, April 1 at 12:24 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Transporter-4, SpaceX’s fourth dedicated smallsat rideshare program mission, from Space Launch Complex 40 at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida. This was the seventh launch and landing of this Falcon 9 stage booster, which previously supported launch of Crew-1, Crew-2, SXM-8, CRS-23, IXPE, and one Starlink mission. Following stage separation, SpaceX landed Falcon 9’s first stage on the Just Read the Instructions droneship, which was stationed in the Atlantic Ocean.

On board this flight were 40 spacecraft, including CubeSats, microsats, picosats, and an orbital transfer vehicle.
