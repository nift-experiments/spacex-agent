December 03, 2013

SES-8 Mission

On Monday, December 3 at 5:41 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the SES-8 mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. This was SpaceX’s first mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit.

This was the first and final flight for the Falcon 9 booster supporting this mission.
