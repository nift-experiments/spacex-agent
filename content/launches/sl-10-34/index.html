June 28, 2025

Starlink Mission

REWATCH

On Saturday, June 28 at 12:26 a.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched 27 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Space Force Station in Florida.

This was the 5th flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched CRS-32, NROL-69, GPS III-7, and now two Starlink missions.
