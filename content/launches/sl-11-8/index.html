January 21, 2025

Starlink Mission

Watch

On Tuesday, January 21 at 7:45 a.m. PT, Falcon 9 launched 27 Starlink satellites to low-Earth orbit from Space Launch Complex 4 East (SLC-4E) at Vandenberg Space Force Base in California.

This was the 10th flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched OneWeb Launch 20 mission, USSF-62, and now eight Starlink missions.
