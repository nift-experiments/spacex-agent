July 25, 2019

CRS-18 Mission

REWATCH

On Wednesday, July 25 at 6:01 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched Dragon’s 18th Commercial Resupply Services mission (CRS-18) to the International Space Station from Space Launch Complex 40 (SLC-40) at Cape Canaveral Air Force Station in Florida. Approximately two days later on Friday, July 27 the spacecraft was captured at 9:11 a.m. ET and then berthed to the space station at 12:01 p.m. ET.

After 30 days, Dragon was released from the orbiting laboratory at 10:59 a.m. ET on Tuesday, August 27 and successfully returned to Earth, splashing down off the coast of Baja, California the same day at 1:20 p.m. PT.

The Dragon spacecraft supporting this mission previously flew CRS-6 and CRS-13 to and from the orbiting laboratory. This was the second flight for the first stage booster supporting this mission, which previously launched CRS-17.
