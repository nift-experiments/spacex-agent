October 30, 2017

Koreasat-5A Mission

Rewatch

On Monday, October 30, at 3:34 p.m. ET, Falcon 9 launched the Koreasat-5A mission to geosynchronous transfer orbit from Launch Complex 39A (LC-39A) at Kennedy Space Center in Florida.

This was the first and only flight for the Falcon 9 first stage booster supporting this mission. It successfully landed on the Of Course I Still Love You droneship.
