September 29, 2008

RatSat Mission

On Monday, September 29 at 11:15 a.m. MHT, Falcon 1 launched the RatSat mission to low-Earth orbit from the SpaceX launch site on Omelek Island in the Marshall Islands.

This was Falcon 1’s first successful mission and the first privately developed liquid fuel rocket to reach low-Earth orbit.
